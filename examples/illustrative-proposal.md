# Recurring office cleaning for Example Design Studio

**Fictional illustration — manually authored, not a NAS execution result or real commercial offer.**

## A clear plan for routine office care

Example Office Cleaning Co. proposes a recurring service built around a fixed checklist, a named scheduling contact and a straightforward monthly plan. The offering gives Example Design Studio a clear description of what each visit covers and a completed checklist after every appointment. [S1: Offering; Workflow]

## Proposed service

The standard plan provides two visits each month. Each visit covers accessible surface dusting, floor vacuuming or mopping, emptying ordinary office waste bins, and cleaning one kitchenette and one restroom. [S2: Standard monthly plan]

Before service begins, both parties confirm the preferred appointment times and office access arrangements. The named scheduling contact provides a defined point of contact for coordinating those arrangements. [S1: Workflow; S2: Standard monthly plan]

## Monthly price and scope

The proposed standard plan is **USD 360 per month** for the two included visits. Hazardous waste, exterior windows, specialist equipment cleaning and additional visits are outside the quoted scope. [S2: Standard monthly plan]

## Next step

Example Design Studio is invited to confirm whether the listed scope meets its needs and discuss suitable appointment times and access arrangements. This draft does not establish a start date, payment schedule or additional contractual terms.
