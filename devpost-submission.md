# Title

Dossier

## One-line Summary

Turn your business documents into a persuasive proposal with facts you can trace.

## Problem

Business information is scattered across company profiles, price lists and existing reports. Producing a coherent proposal requires collecting relevant facts without inventing support.

## Solution

A Uliya skill orchestrates authorized NAS document discovery, evidence extraction, persuasive drafting, factual review and approved text saving.

## Why This Matters

The workflow keeps supporting material private and makes proposal claims traceable to the user's selected documents.
